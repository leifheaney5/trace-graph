# Technical review workflow

The Reviews workspace turns seeded or newly created `ReviewSession` artifacts
into an auditable technical-review record. A session has a chair, meeting date,
disposition, status, agenda, and canonical `reviews` links to requirements,
risks, constraints, decisions, or other model elements. Completing a review
updates the session status without copying or disconnecting the reviewed
artifacts. Formal minutes, action-owner tracking, and electronic approvals are
future extensions.
