# TraceGraph Messaging Guide

## One-line description

TraceGraph is a local-first requirements engineering and digital-thread workbench that turns stakeholder intent into inspectable engineering evidence.

## Elevator pitch

TraceGraph connects the engineering chain from stakeholder intent through requirements, architecture, verification, evidence, change impact, and baselines in one canonical model. Instead of hiding engineering state behind a generic dashboard score, it lets users inspect where information came from, why artifacts are related, whether evidence is still valid, and exactly how a proposed change propagates downstream.

## Primary value propositions

1. **Trace the reasoning, not just the records.** Preserve source, rationale, direction, confidence, provenance, review, and baseline context.
2. **Understand change before committing it.** Follow explainable direct and transitive impact paths through the digital thread.
3. **Separate evidence existence from evidence validity.** Surface stale, incomplete, review-needed, and superseded evidence states.
4. **Keep one source of engineering truth.** Requirements, architecture, verification, impact, and baseline views remain projections over canonical artifacts.
5. **Review it anywhere.** The guest demo is local-first, deterministic, and ships with a rich synthetic project.

## Audience-specific framing

### Systems / requirements engineer

"See the complete provenance and downstream consequences of a requirement without reconstructing the thread manually."

### Verification engineer

"Connect verification cases and evidence to the exact requirement version they support, then see when that support becomes stale."

### Technical lead

"Review change impact and baseline divergence with the relationship path and supporting engineering context visible."

### Portfolio / case-study reviewer

"Explore a deterministic, inspectable digital-engineering workflow without proprietary data or external services."

## Claims to avoid

Do not describe TraceGraph as certified MBSE tooling, standards-complete SysML/UML software, a deployed customer system, a compliance solution, an AI system that autonomously changes canonical engineering data, or proof of measured productivity/revenue/uptime improvements.

## Preferred language

Use: **inspectable**, **canonical**, **evidence-backed**, **explainable impact**, **local-first**, **deterministic**, **synthetic demo**, **practical projection**, **structural trace query**.

Avoid: **certified**, **fully compliant**, **production-proven**, **autonomous engineering**, **enterprise-ready** unless independently substantiated outside this repository.
