## LinkedIn-style post

I’ve finished a case-study-ready pass on TraceGraph, a local-first requirements engineering and digital-thread workbench.

The product is centered on a simple idea: engineering traceability should explain the thread, not just display records. TraceGraph keeps stakeholder source material, requirements, architecture allocations, verification, evidence, change impact, and baselines connected through one canonical model.

A few areas I focused on:

- evidence validity rather than simple evidence presence;
- explainable direct and transitive change-impact paths;
- deterministic structural trace queries that refuse unsupported questions;
- version-aware lifecycle, reviews, change requests, and baselines;
- explicit provenance, rationale, confidence, and relationship direction;
- a local-first deterministic demo that does not depend on proprietary data or a hosted AI model.

The repository includes a synthetic Emergency Response Drone project, a browser-recorded walkthrough, 13 product screenshots, automated accessibility coverage, and reproducible test/build evidence.

TraceGraph is deliberately presented as an inspectable engineering workbench, not as certified enterprise MBSE tooling or a standards-complete SysML/UML editor.
